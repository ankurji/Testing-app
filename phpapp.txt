  What it does
  - Dashboard: spending today, this week and this month, plus income, net, total
    EMIs, loan balance remaining, savings, and a category breakdown.
  - Expenses: add expenses with a category and note. You can view them by day,
    week, month or year, and step back and forward through periods.
  - Income: the same daily, weekly, monthly and yearly views for income.
  - Loans: enter the principal, interest rate and tenure, and it works out the
    EMI. You can record payments and see a progress bar and the remaining
    amount.
  - Savings goals: set a target and deadline, and add deposits as you save.

  Amounts are stored as whole paise/cents, so there's no rounding drift. Delete
  and add actions use CSRF tokens.

  The remaining loan amount is a simple EMI × tenure − paid, not a true
  interest-aware outstanding balance.
